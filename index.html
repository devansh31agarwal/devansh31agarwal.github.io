Mathematical interests

Three areas of mathematics intrigue me most deeply.

Combinatorics
The study of how things can be counted, arranged and combined, and why the answers take the shape they do.
Probability
Reasoning about chance and uncertainty with the same rigour as any other branch of mathematics.
Linear Algebra
Vector spaces, linear maps and matrices, the structure that runs beneath so much of modern mathematics.
